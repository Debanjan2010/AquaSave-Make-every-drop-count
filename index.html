Easy ideas to get started

Saving water can be simple

🚿

Shorten one shower

Try trimming a minute or two. Small daily habits can add up across the whole household.

🪣

Reuse water safely

Use suitable leftover water for plants. Avoid water that contains strong cleaners or chemicals.

🌧️

Let rain help

Check the forecast before watering. A rain barrel can collect roof runoff where local rules allow.

A quick note: AquaSave is an educational prototype. Savings are estimates based on typical water use, and weather information depends on the forecast service and your internet connection.
